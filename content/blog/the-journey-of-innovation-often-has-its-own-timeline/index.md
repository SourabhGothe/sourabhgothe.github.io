A patent originally filed in December 2019 — **"Method and Electronic Device for Managing Romanized Abugida Input"** — has been officially granted by the **Indian Patent Office**. 🎉

The invention addresses a long-standing challenge in mobile text input: inconsistent spellings when typing Abugida-script languages (such as Bengali, Hindi, Kannada, and Thai) using Romanized characters. Prior to this work, the lack of standardized Romanized forms led to frustrating autocorrect errors and poor next-word predictions.

The patented system dynamically disambiguates the multiple Romanized variants users may type for the same word, significantly improving keyboard experience on smartphones.

**Co-inventors:** Chandramouli Sanchi, Yellappa Damam, Sharmila Mani, and Sourabh Gothe — developed at **Samsung R&D Institute India – Bangalore (SRIB)**.

This recognition marks an important milestone, transforming early-stage exploratory research into a formally recognized intellectual property.
