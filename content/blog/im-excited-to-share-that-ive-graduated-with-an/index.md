Sourabh Gothe has completed his **M.Tech in Data Science and Business Analytics** from the prestigious **Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bangalore**, with a GPA of 9.3 — sponsored by **Samsung R&D Institute India (SRIB)** as part of its commitment to fostering continuous learning among its engineers.

The two-year programme encompassed advanced coursework in machine learning, computer vision, and data systems under leading faculty including Prof. Deepak Subramani, Prof. Prathosh AP, Prof. Yogesh Simmhan, Prof. Venkatesh Babu, Prof. Sashikumaar Ganesan, and Prof. Shashi Jain.

The opportunity was made possible through SRIB's leadership — Dipesh Shah, Mohan Rao, and Sreevatsa D B — and the Learning & Development team's support in fostering a culture of professional growth.
