India hosted the **50th edition of the IEEE International Conference on Acoustics, Speech, and Signal Processing (ICASSP 2025)** in Hyderabad — a landmark milestone for the global signal processing and AI research community, drawing over 4,000 researchers from across the world.

**Samsung R&D Institute India – Bangalore (SRIB)** made a strong showing at the conference, with **six researchers** presenting papers spanning speech and audio, image processing, and video processing. Additionally, Sudha Velusamy and Lokesh Boregowda served as session chairs — a testament to the depth of research talent at SRIB.

**SRIB presenters at ICASSP 2025:** Amit Chaulwar, Rishabh Gupta, Kunal Swami, Sandeep Agri, Prince Arya, Chelamkuri Omsrinath, **Sourabh Gothe**, and Sowmya Lahari Vajrala.
