**Samsung R&D Institute India – Bangalore (SRIB)** received the **Best Practice Award** from **Samsung Research-Korea**, recognizing the institute's decade-long track record of publishing impactful research at top global technology conferences and journals.

The award reflects the consistent dedication of SRIB's research community and the leadership vision that has made high-quality publications a core institutional priority. The achievement was celebrated with a team gathering, bringing together researchers and leaders in a moment of shared pride.

**Senior leaders acknowledged:** Dipesh Shah, Mohan Rao, Dr. Balvinder Singh, Dr. Aloknath De, Ajit Bopardikar, PhD, and Amritesh Srivastava.
