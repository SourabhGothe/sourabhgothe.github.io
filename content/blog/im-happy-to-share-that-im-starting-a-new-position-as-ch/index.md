Sourabh Gothe has been promoted to the role of **Chief Engineer** at **Samsung R&D Institute India – Bangalore (SRIB)**.

This milestone reflects over nine years of contributions to cutting-edge research and development in on-device AI, NLP, and computer vision at Samsung — spanning roles from Software Engineer through Lead Engineer to this latest appointment.
