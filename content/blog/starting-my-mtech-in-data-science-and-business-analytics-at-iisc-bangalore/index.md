Sourabh Gothe enrolled in the **M.Tech in Data Science and Business Analytics** programme at the **Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bangalore** — one of India's premier research institutions — sponsored by **Samsung R&D Institute India (SRIB)** through its employee learning and development initiative.

The two-year programme covers advanced topics in machine learning, computer vision, data systems, and statistical modelling, taught by leading faculty. The sponsorship reflects SRIB's commitment to investing in the continuous technical growth of its engineers and enabling world-class applied research.
