Sourabh Gothe has been honored with the **"Eminent Author of the Year"** award at **Samsung R&D Institute India – Bangalore (SRIB)** — an annual recognition presented for exceptional contributions to research and publication at prestigious international venues.

The recognition highlights work presented at top conferences including **WACV** and **ICASSP**, achieved through close collaboration with teammates Jayesh Vachhani, Vibhav Agarwal, Sourav Ghosh, Rishabh Khurana, and Pranay Kashyap.

The award reflects the research culture nurtured by SRIB leadership — Jayesh Vachhani, Parameswaranath VM, Barath Raj KR, Sreevatsa D B, CTO Mohan Rao, and MD Dipesh Shah — and the academic rigour developed during M.Tech studies at **IISc Bangalore** under Prof. Deepak Subramani and Prof. Prathosh AP.
