Sourabh Gothe was promoted to the role of **Lead Engineer** at **Samsung R&D Institute India – Bangalore (SRIB)**, following several years of contributions to on-device AI and natural language processing research.

In this role, he took on expanded responsibilities in driving technical direction for the NLP and on-device AI team, including leading research projects, mentoring engineers, and coordinating publication efforts at top international venues.
