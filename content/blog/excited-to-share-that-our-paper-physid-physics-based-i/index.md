The research paper **"PhysID: Physics-Based Interactive Dynamics From A Single-View Image"** received the **Bronze Award** at the **Samsung Best Paper Awards (SBPA 2024)** — a prestigious recognition program celebrating innovative research across Samsung R&D centers worldwide. 🏆

The paper tackles the challenging problem of enabling interactive physics-based dynamics from a single-view image. The approach combines **Large Reconstruction Models (LRM)**, **Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs)**, and a **Physics-Based Rendering engine** to automate a traditionally labor-intensive pipeline into a scalable, end-to-end solution.

**Authors:** Sourabh Gothe, Ayon Chattopadhyay, Phani Kiran Gunturi Venkata Sai, Vibhav Agarwal, Jayesh R Vachhani, and Sourav Ghosh — developed at **Samsung R&D Institute India – Bangalore (SRIB)**.
