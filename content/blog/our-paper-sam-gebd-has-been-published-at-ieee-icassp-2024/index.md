The paper **"SAM-GEBD: Zero-Cost Approach for Generic Event Boundary Detection"** was published at **IEEE ICASSP 2024**.

The work proposes a novel, **training-free** approach to Generic Event Boundary Detection (GEBD) that leverages rich visual features extracted from the **Segment Anything Model (SAM)** — a large-scale foundation model — without requiring any task-specific fine-tuning or labelled video data. The method achieves highly competitive detection performance at zero training cost, demonstrating the transferability of large vision models to temporal video understanding tasks.

**Authors:** Pranay Kashyap, Sourabh Vasant Gothe, Jayesh Vachhani, Vibhav Agarwal — developed at **Samsung R&D Institute India – Bangalore (SRIB)**.
