Sourabh Gothe attended and presented research at the **2025 IEEE International Conference on Acoustics, Speech and Signal Processing (ICASSP 2025)**, marking the team's **third consecutive year** publishing at this flagship conference.

The event brought together leading researchers from academia, industry, and startups worldwide, providing a platform for engaging discussions on cutting-edge advances in signal processing, speech, and AI. The conference was particularly meaningful as a continued milestone in the team's growing presence in the global research community.
