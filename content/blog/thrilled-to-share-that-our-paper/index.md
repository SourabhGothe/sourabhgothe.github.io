Our paper **"ContextGraph: Lifelog Intelligence Framework for Contextual Subgraph Evolution"** has been accepted to the **AAAI 2026 Main Technical Track** — one of the most prestigious venues in Artificial Intelligence research.

The work introduces a novel lifelog intelligence framework that models contextual knowledge as evolving subgraphs, enabling richer and more personalized AI reasoning over long-term user context.

This milestone is the result of exceptional team collaboration and reflects the commitment to advancing research in agentic memory and contextual intelligence.
