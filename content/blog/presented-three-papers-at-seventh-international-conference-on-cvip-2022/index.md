The research team at **Samsung R&D Institute India – Bangalore (SRIB)** presented three papers at the **Seventh International Conference on Computer Vision & Image Processing (CVIP 2022)**:

**1. "SAMNet: Semantic Aware Multimodal Network for Emoji Drawing Classification"**
A multimodal deep neural network that jointly learns from text and freehand drawing inputs to classify emojis — enabling richer, more expressive interactions on mobile devices.
*Authors: Sourabh Vasant Gothe, Rishabh Khurana, Jayesh Vachhani, Rakshith S, Pranay Kashyap.*

**2. "Self Similarity Matrix based CNN Filter Pruning"**
A structured network compression method that uses self-similarity analysis of convolutional filter activations to identify and prune redundant filters — reducing model size while preserving accuracy.
*Authors: Rakshith S, Jayesh Vachhani, Sourabh Vasant Gothe, Rishabh Khurana.*

**3. "Class Agnostic, On-Device and Privacy Preserving Repetition Counting of Actions from Videos using Similarity Bottleneck"**
A privacy-preserving, class-agnostic framework for counting repetitive actions in video streams, designed for efficient on-device deployment without requiring cloud processing or action-specific training.
*Authors: Rishabh Khurana, Jayesh Vachhani, Sourabh Vasant Gothe, Rakshith S.*
