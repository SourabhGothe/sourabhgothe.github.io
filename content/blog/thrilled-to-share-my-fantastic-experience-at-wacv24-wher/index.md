Sourabh Gothe presented research on **unsupervised Generic Event Boundary Detection (GEBD)** during the poster session at **WACV 2024** — the IEEE/CVF Winter Conference on Applications of Computer Vision.

The paper, **"What's in the Flow? Exploiting Temporal Motion Cues for Unsupervised Generic Event Boundary Detection"**, introduces a lightweight approach that leverages optical flow-based temporal motion cues to detect event boundaries in videos without requiring labelled training data.

The conference provided a valuable opportunity to engage with fellow researchers and practitioners, discuss the method's applicability and edge cases, and explore potential future collaborations in computer vision.
