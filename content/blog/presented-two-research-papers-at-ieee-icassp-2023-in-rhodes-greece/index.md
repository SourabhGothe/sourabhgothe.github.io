The research team at **Samsung R&D Institute India – Bangalore (SRIB)** presented two papers at the **48th IEEE International Conference on Acoustics, Speech, and Signal Processing (ICASSP 2023)** in Rhodes, Greece:

**1. "Self-Similarity Is All You Need For Fast And Light-Weight Generic Event Boundary Detection"**
A fast, lightweight framework for Generic Event Boundary Detection (GEBD) that leverages self-similarity of video frame features to detect temporal boundaries — achieving real-time performance without requiring large-scale supervised training.
*Authors: Sourabh Vasant Gothe, Jayesh Vachhani, Rishabh Khurana, Pranay Kashyap.*

**2. "Repetition Counting From Compressed Videos Using Sparse Residual Similarity"**
A resource-efficient method for counting action repetitions directly within the compressed video domain, using sparse residual similarity signals — bypassing the need for full video decoding and enabling on-device deployment.
*Authors: Rishabh Khurana, Jayesh Vachhani, Sourabh Vasant Gothe, Pranay Kashyap.*

The conference, held in the historic city of Rhodes, brought together leading signal processing and AI researchers from across the world.
